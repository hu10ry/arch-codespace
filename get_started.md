# Get started

## Navigation
`Ctrl-E` Opens up rofi, your main way of navigation.

## Changing themes
You can theme openbox with the Openbox Settings app in rofi (or obconf-qt in the shell)
You can theme everything QT with "Customize look and feel" (lxappearance)

Wallpaper is defined in ~/.config/desktop-env.sh
$THEME has no use as of now


(You can remove this auto opening by removing the final line of ~/.config/openbox/autostart.sh)
